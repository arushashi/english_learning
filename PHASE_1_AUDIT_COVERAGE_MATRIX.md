---

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA (Applied Strictly)

A topic is marked **Present** ONLY if ALL of these are true:
- ✅ Has its own lesson page reachable from the learning path
- ✅ Explanation is in Kannada, not English
- ✅ Has at least 8 example sentences in three-line format (English / transliteration / Kannada)
- ✅ Has audio (or audio infrastructure in place)
- ✅ Has at least 10 practice questions with instant feedback
- ✅ Has "common mistakes for Kannada speakers" section
- ✅ Is assigned a CEFR level (A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2)

Anything else = **Partial** or **Missing**.
