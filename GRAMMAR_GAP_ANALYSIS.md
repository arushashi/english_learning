## KANNADA INTERFERENCE PATTERNS TO ADDRESS

### Common Mistakes (Kannada Speakers)

1. **Word Order**
   - Kannada: SOV (Subject-Object-Verb)
   - English: SVO (Subject-Verb-Object)
   - Example: "I book read" instead of "I read a book"

2. **Articles**
   - Kannada has no articles
   - Overuse or omission of A/An/The
   - Example: "I go to school" vs "I go to the school"

3. **Verb Tenses**
   - Kannada has fewer tense distinctions
   - Confusion between simple past and present perfect
   - Overuse of present tense

4. **Prepositions**
   - Different preposition usage
   - Example: "In Monday" instead of "On Monday"

5. **Subject Omission**
   - Kannada allows subject omission
   - English requires explicit subject
   - Example: "Is good" instead of "It is good"

6. **Plural Markers**
   - Kannada plurals are optional
   - English requires -s/-es
   - Example: "Two book" instead of "Two books"
