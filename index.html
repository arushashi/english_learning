Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to complete the course?

With consistent daily practice (30-60 minutes), you can complete all 10 levels in 90 days. However, you can learn at your own pace.

Do I need to register or pay?

No! This course is completely free and requires no registration. Your progress is automatically saved in your browser.

Can I skip levels?

Yes, you can start at any level. However, we recommend starting from Level 0 for the best learning experience.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes! You'll earn certificates upon completing each level and the entire course.

What if I need help?

All lessons include Kannada explanations. You can also review any lesson multiple times.
