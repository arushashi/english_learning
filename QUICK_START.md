# Quick Start Guide - Kannada Spoken English Website

## Getting Started in 3 Steps

### Step 1: Open the Website
1. Navigate to the `english_learning` folder
2. Double-click `index.html`
3. The website opens in your default browser
4. **No installation or registration needed!**

### Step 2: Choose Your Starting Level
- **Absolute Beginner?** → Start with **Level 0** (Alphabet & Phonics)
- **Know some English?** → Start with **Level 1** (Survival English)
- **Want to skip ahead?** → Click any level directly

### Step 3: Start Learning
1. Click "Start Learning Now" on the home page
2. Select a level from the Levels page
3. Read the lessons with Kannada explanations
4. Practice speaking using the Practice page
5. Track your progress in My Progress

## How to Practice

### 1. Reading & Understanding
- Read each lesson carefully
- Look at Kannada explanations (in orange)
- Understand the grammar and vocabulary

### 2. Listening
- Click the audio button to hear pronunciation
- Listen to native speakers
- Repeat after them

### 3. Speaking
- Go to "Practice" page
- Record yourself speaking
- Compare with native audio
- Practice daily

### 4. Testing
- Take quizzes after each lesson
- Check your understanding
- Review weak areas

---

## Daily Learning Routine (30-60 minutes)

### Morning (15 minutes)
- Read one lesson
- Listen to pronunciation
- Repeat 5 times

### Afternoon (15 minutes)
- Practice speaking on the Practice page
- Record yourself
- Listen and review

### Evening (15-30 minutes)
- Take a quiz
- Review mistakes
- Plan next lesson

---

## Tips for Success

### ✅ Do This
- **Practice daily** - Even 15 minutes is better than 2 hours once a week
- **Speak aloud** - Don't just read silently
- **Use Kannada** - Refer to Kannada explanations when confused
- **Record yourself** - Listen to your progress
- **Complete each level** - Don't skip levels
- **Be patient** - Learning takes time

### ❌ Don't Do This
- Don't translate word-by-word
- Don't skip levels
- Don't practice only grammar
- Don't be afraid of mistakes
- Don't expect overnight results
- Don't give up

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Do I need to register?
**A:** No! Start learning immediately without any registration.

### Q: Can I skip levels?
**A:** Yes, but we recommend starting from Level 0 for best results.

### Q: How long does it take?

### Q: Will I get a certificate?
