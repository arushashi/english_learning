### GAP 1.1: ARTICLES (a/an/the) — CRITICAL PRIORITY

**Impact:** Highest. Kannada has no articles; this is the #1 error source.  
**Learner Error Examples:**
- "I am having a car" (wrong — stative verb in continuous)
- "I go to school" (missing article — should be "the school" in context)
- "She is teacher" (missing article — should be "a teacher")

**Proposed Lesson Structure:**

**Lesson Title:** "Articles: a, an, the, and Zero Article"  
**CEFR Level:** A1  
**Kannada Title:** "ಲೇಖನಗಳು: a, an, the ಮತ್ತು ಶೂನ್ಯ ಲೇಖನ"

**Lesson Components:**

1. **Objective (in Kannada)**
   - "ಈ ಪಾಠದಲ್ಲಿ ನೀವು ಕಲಿಯುವುದು: ಕಾರ್ಯಕ್ಷೇತ್ರದಲ್ಲಿ 'a', 'an', 'the' ಮತ್ತು ಶೂನ್ಯ ಲೇಖನ ಬಳಸುವುದು."

2. **Concept Explanation (in Kannada)**
   - Contrast: Kannada has no articles. English requires them.
   - Kannada: "ಅವಳು ಶಿಕ್ಷಕೆ" (no article needed)
   - English: "She is **a** teacher" (article required)

3. **8–10 Example Sentences** (three-line format)
   ```
   English: I have a car.
   Transliteration: Aai hav ə kaar.
   Kannada: ನನಗೆ ಒಂದು ಕಾರು ಇದೆ.
   
   English: The sun is bright.
   Transliteration: Ðə sun iz braait.
   Kannada: ಸೂರ್ಯ ಪ್ರಕಾಶಮಾನವಾಗಿದೆ.
   
   English: She is an engineer.
   Transliteration: Shii iz ən en-jin-iir.
   Kannada: ಅವಳು ಇಂಜಿನಿಯರ್.
   
   English: I like coffee.
   Transliteration: Ai laik kaa-fii.
   Kannada: ನನಗೆ ಕಾಫಿ ಇಷ್ಟ.
   
   English: The book is on the table.
   Transliteration: Ðə buk iz on ðə tei-bəl.
   Kannada: ಪುಸ್ತಕ ಮೇಜಿನ ಮೇಲೆ ಇದೆ.
   
   English: An apple a day keeps the doctor away.
   Transliteration: Ən ap-əl ə dei kiips ðə dok-tər ə-wei.
   Kannada: ಪ್ರತಿದಿನ ಒಂದು ಸೇಬು ವೈದ್ಯರನ್ನು ದೂರ ಇಡುತ್ತದೆ.
   
   English: Water is essential for life.
   Transliteration: Waa-tər iz i-sen-shəl for laif.
   Kannada: ನೀರು ಜೀವನಕ್ಕೆ ಅಗತ್ಯ.
   
   English: I saw a movie yesterday.
   Transliteration: Ai saa ə muu-vii yes-tər-dei.
   Kannada: ನಾನು ನಿನ್ನೆ ಒಂದು ಚಲನಚಿತ್ರ ನೋಡಿದೆ.
   
   English: The Taj Mahal is in Agra.
   Transliteration: Ðə taj mə-haal iz in ag-rə.
   Kannada: ತಾಜ್ ಮಹಾಲ್ ಆಗ್ರಾದಲ್ಲಿ ಇದೆ.
   
   English: She has a degree in engineering.
   Transliteration: Shii haz ə di-grii in en-jin-iir-ing.
   Kannada: ಅವಳು ಇಂಜಿನಿಯರಿಂಗ್‌ನಲ್ಲಿ ಪದವಿ ಹೊಂದಿದ್ದಾಳೆ.
   ```

4. **Audio** (native speaker or high-quality TTS, plus slow-speed option)

5. **Guided Practice** (10+ questions)
   - Fill-in-the-blank: "I have ___ car." (a/an/the/nothing)
   - MCQ: "Which is correct? (A) She is teacher (B) She is a teacher (C) She is the teacher"
   - Sentence rearrange: "car / a / I / have" → "I have a car"
   - Error spotting: "The water is important" (correct) vs. "Water is important" (also correct — explain both)
   - Match-the-pair: Match sentences with correct article usage

6. **Common Mistakes for Kannada Speakers**
   - ❌ "I am having a car" → ✅ "I have a car" (not continuous; stative verb)
   - ❌ "I go to school" → ✅ "I go to **the** school" (context-dependent)
   - ❌ "She is teacher" → ✅ "She is **a** teacher"
   - ❌ "I like tea" → ✅ "I like **tea**" (generic; no article) OR "I like **the** tea" (specific)
   - ❌ "I saw movie" → ✅ "I saw **a** movie"

7. **Quiz with Instant Feedback** (5 questions)
   - Feedback in Kannada for wrong answers
   - Example: "ತಪ್ಪು! ಕಾರಣ: 'teacher' ಎಂಬ ಪದವು ಗಣನೀಯ ಬಹುವಚನ ಹೆಸರು. ಅದು ಮೊದಲ ಬಾರಿ ಉಲ್ಲೇಖಿಸಿದಾಗ 'a' ಅಗತ್ಯ."

**Where It Fits:** Level 1 (after "Greetings" and "Self-Introduction"), as a foundational grammar lesson.

**Estimated Effort:** 20 hours (content creation, audio, practice questions, feedback).

### GAP 1.2: KANNADA INTERFERENCE MODULE (10 Lessons)

**Impact:** Highest. Addresses the root causes of Kannada speaker errors.

**Proposed Lessons:**

1. **Articles (a/an/the)** — See GAP 1.1 above.

2. **Word Order: SOV (Kannada) vs. SVO (English)**
   - Kannada: "ರಮೇಶ್ ಅವನು ಕಾರು ಚಾಲನೆ ಮಾಡುತ್ತಾನೆ" (Ramesh he car drive does) — SOV
   - English: "Ramesh drives a car" — SVO
   - Lesson: 8+ examples, 10 practice Qs, common mistakes, audio.

3. **Stative Verbs in Continuous Tense**
   - ❌ "I am having a car" → ✅ "I have a car"
   - ❌ "I am knowing him" → ✅ "I know him"
   - Lesson: Identify stative verbs, explain why no continuous, 8+ examples, 10 practice Qs.

4. **Tag Questions (ಅಲ್ವಾ)**
   - Kannada: "ನೀವು ಇಂಗ್ಲಿಷ್ ಮಾತನಾಡುತ್ತೀರಿ, ಅಲ್ವಾ?" (You speak English, isn't it?)
   - English: "You speak English, **don't you**?" (NOT "isn't it?")
   - Lesson: Tag question formation, 8+ examples, 10 practice Qs, common mistakes.

5. **Overuse of "Only" & "Itself" (from ಅಷ್ಟೇ / ಅದೇ)**
   - ❌ "I am only a student" → ✅ "I am just a student" (or simply "I am a student")
   - ❌ "The book itself is good" → ✅ "The book is good" (or "The book itself is interesting" if emphasis needed)
   - Lesson: When to use "only," "just," "itself," 8+ examples, 10 practice Qs.

6. **Indianisms: When Acceptable, When Not**
   - "What is your good name?" — Acceptable in India, but "What is your name?" is standard.
   - "Do the needful" — Acceptable in Indian English, but "Please do what is necessary" is more standard.
   - "Kindly revert" — Acceptable in Indian English, but "Please reply" is more standard.
   - Lesson: Explain each, provide alternatives, 8+ examples, 10 practice Qs.

7. **Postpositions (Kannada) vs. Prepositions (English)**
   - Kannada: "ಮೇಜಿನ ಮೇಲೆ" (table-on) — postposition
   - English: "on the table" — preposition
   - Lesson: Common postpositions, their English equivalents, 8+ examples, 10 practice Qs.

8. **Pronunciation Interference**
   - v/w: "very" vs. "wery"
   - f/p: "coffee" vs. "kaapi"
   - sh/s: "she" vs. "see"
   - Retroflex t/d vs. English alveolar
   - Vowel insertion: "school" → "ish-kool"
   - Lesson: Minimal pairs, pronunciation drills, audio at slow speed, 10 practice Qs.

9. **Gender-Neutral ಅವರು vs. he/she/they**
   - Kannada: "ಅವರು" (they/he/she — gender-neutral)
   - English: Must choose he, she, or they
   - Lesson: Pronoun agreement, 8+ examples, 10 practice Qs.

10. **Missing Plural -s & Third-Person -s**
    - ❌ "He go to school" → ✅ "He goes to school"
    - ❌ "I have two book" → ✅ "I have two books"
    - Lesson: Plural rules, third-person singular -s, 8+ examples, 10 practice Qs.

**Where It Fits:** Interspersed throughout Levels 1–3, with a dedicated "Kannada Interference" section in the UI.

**Estimated Effort:** 150 hours (10 lessons × 15 hours each).
