   - Lists 17 stative verbs (have, know, like, love, hate, want, need, believe, understand, see, hear, taste, smell, feel, seem, appear, be)

   - Addresses Kannada overuse of "ಅಲ್ವಾ" (isn't it?)

   - 10 example sentences with correct usage

5. **Indianisms - When Acceptable, When Not**
   - Covers "good name", "do the needful", "kindly revert", "presently", "having a doubt"
   - Provides standard English alternatives
   - 10 example sentences with corrections
